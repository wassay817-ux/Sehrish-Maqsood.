About

Introduction, approach, and performance marketing philosophy.

Sehrish is a performance marketer and digital strategist based in Sargodha, Punjab, with eight-plus years building and running campaigns for B2B and B2C brands across more than ten industries. Her work spans lead generation, paid social, content strategy, and full marketing-department management — usually all at once.

She's co-founder and general manager at Techsole System, where she runs a cross-functional team of marketers, designers, and SEO specialists serving clients across the UAE, USA, UK, and Malaysia. Recent work includes leading marketing and design for a medical symposium in Dubai and running lead-generation strategy for an appliance-repair brand across a 500+ prospect list.

Her approach is unapologetically data-driven: competitor mapping before creative, device-level bid adjustments, A/B tested ad copy, and a hard focus on cost-per-lead and ROAS over vanity metrics.
